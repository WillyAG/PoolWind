# Commercial use

PoolWind is free for personal, hobby and other noncommercial use under the
licenses described in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

**Any commercial use requires a separate written license from the author.**
Examples:

- Selling PoolWind kits, interface boards, or assembled systems
- Selling pools, pool equipment, or accessories that include PoolWind or code,
  designs or documentation derived from it
- Installing, configuring or supporting PoolWind for pay
- Offering PoolWind features as part of a paid product or service
- Coaches, swim schools, clubs or training programs using PoolWind to train
  paying swimmers, for example charging for sessions in a PoolWind pool or
  for stroke analysis with its swim plot and training video

Commercial licenses are available, including to pool manufacturers, to
makers who want to offer ready-built systems, and to coaches and training
programs.
