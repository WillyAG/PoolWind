What's new in PoolWind v15.7 (since v15.5)

For swimmers (user manual):
- Your own Pace table on your USB stick, made from the pool's the first
  time the stick is used. Pace correction changes yours; other swimmers
  aren't affected.
- Pace correction redone: correct your Paces while you swim. Changes stay
  pending until you press Make permanent. "Copy Master values" tries the
  pool's own Paces.
- A USB stick plugged in or pulled out while PoolWind runs is picked up.
  The stick is always ejected when PoolWind closes.
- Swim Plot and the calendar show old swims in the Paces in use now. The
  first start after this update adds the jet's voltage to your old swim
  logs, once for each USB stick (a few seconds). Days that can't be
  converted show grey.
- Swim Plot: "Use: Swimmer dataset / Master dataset (coach)" on the date
  screen, so a coach can compare swimmers on the pool's table; a "Please
  wait" box while swims are read, and what's been read is kept;
  goto MPH / goto Pace, PACE or MPH beside the date, a coordinate box with
  its legend; Compress Gaps keeps the cursor and the values shown.
- Swimmer limits also sets your height, training video and its frames per
  second (Manual Settings), and so does a new stick's setup.

For the builder (setup guide, section 5):
- The voltage scan can be run again any time; its Paces are a first
  estimate (7:00 at the slowest step, 1:00 at the top). It checks that
  the pool's voltage changes in steps: a pool whose voltage rises
  continuously needs changes (see the setup guide and Claude).
- Pace Entry has Edit, Auto scan and Finish screens; PingPong has bodyDrag
  and deltaBodyDrag. Both can go on into the steps above the table's top.
- The jet's limits are now the Pace table's first and last rows. An old
  'SafetyVoltageLimits' line in the calibration file is ignored.
- The shipped settings use zones, 'zoneMin, Max': (6, 12), in place of a
  Pace range.
- The jet winds down to its lowest when PoolWind starts and closes, and
  when Set Swim Area or the calibration opens; for Swim Plot to the
  swimmer's slowest zone. It saves wear on the equipment.
- A settings or calibration file PoolWind can't start with, a misspelled
  setting name or a wrong word: a box shows the file, the line and the
  problem. No voltage sensor: a box says what to check.
- A new install on the Pi is no longer a demo: PoolWind controls the pool
  from its first start (the demo is on a Windows PC). An updated
  installation keeps its 'Demo' line as it is.
