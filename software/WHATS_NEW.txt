What's new in PoolWind v15.7 (since v15.5)

For swimmers (user manual):
- Your own Pace table on your USB stick. A welcome screen greets you
  and says which table you swim with; when the pool is calibrated
  again, it asks Old (yours) or New (the pool's).
- A USB stick plugged in or pulled out while PoolWind runs is picked up.
- Pace correction redone: correct your Paces while you swim. Changes
  stay pending until you make them permanent.
- Swim Plot and the calendar show old swims in today's Paces. The
  first start after this update adds the jet's voltage to your old swim
  logs, once for each USB stick (a few seconds). Days that can't be
  converted show grey.

For the builder (setup guide, section 5):
- The voltage scan can be run again any time; its Paces are a first
  estimate (7:00 at the slowest speed, 1:00 at the top).
- Pace Entry has Edit, Scan and Finish screens; PingPong has bodyDrag
  and deltaBodyDrag.
- The jet's limits are now the Pace table's first and last rows. An old
  'SafetyVoltageLimits' line in the calibration file is ignored.
